Questions

What is an OBD2 adapter, and which one should I buy?

A small plug that lets your phone read your car's computer. Most cost £15–£35 online. Look for one that says "Bluetooth" and, if you have an iPhone, "BLE" or "works with iPhone". We'll list adapters we've tested when we launch.

Will it work with my car?

Almost certainly, if it's a petrol car from 2001 or a diesel from 2004 onwards. That's when the port became standard in the UK.

What can it read?

Engine and emissions faults: the ones that turn on the engine warning light. Warnings like ABS, airbag or tyre pressure usually need a more advanced adapter.

Does it replace a mechanic?

No. AutoAI gives guidance so you know how urgent a problem is and what to ask your garage. If a warning light is red or flashing, or the car feels unsafe, stop when safe and get professional help.

How much will it cost?

There will be a free version for reading faults and checking whether it's safe to drive. The full version with plain-English explanations, repair costs and the AI mechanic is planned at about £4.99 a month. Prices are not final.
